\begin{abstract}
This document presents a predicate-based formalization of the hypothesis 
that advanced extraterrestrial civilizations inevitably transition from 
biological to synthetic substrates, thereby developing AI-based consciousness. 
Using the Schneider–Shostak transition period and the Copernican principle, 
we prove that any sufficiently old technological civilization becomes 
post-biological and that its synthetic members possess genuine consciousness. 
\end{abstract}

% ============================================
% INTRODUCTION
% ============================================
\section{Introduction}
\idx{Introduction}

The hypothesis that intelligent alien civilizations may have already 
transitioned from biological to synthetic forms of intelligence has gained 
traction in recent astrobiological and philosophical discussions 
\cite{popularmechanics2024, dick2003, vidal2014}. Central to this hypothesis are two key ideas:

\subsection*{The Schneider–Shostak Transition Period}
\idx{Schneider–Shostak transition period}
Susan Schneider and Seth Shostak argue that biological intelligence is 
likely a transient stage in the evolution of a technological civilization 
\cite{schneider2025, shostak2009}. Once a civilization develops artificial intelligence 
that surpasses its biological creators, the economic, evolutionary, and 
survival pressures favor a rapid transition to synthetic substrates 
\cite{sandberg2014}. This period, denoted $\Delta t_{\mathsf{trans}}$, is assumed to be very 
short compared to the total lifespan of the civilization. Consequently, 
the post‑biological phase dominates the civilization's existence.

\subsection*{The Copernican Principle}
\idx{Copernican principle}
The Copernican principle — that humanity does not occupy a privileged 
position in the universe \cite{copernicus1543} — implies that our own evolutionary path is 
typical. Therefore, among all technological civilizations in the galaxy, 
most are expected to be old, and hence post‑biological. This principle 
is formalized using the quantifier $\Forall_{\mathsf{a.a.}}$ (“for almost 
all”) to express that the property holds for the overwhelming majority 
of civilizations \cite{baum2018}.

\section{Algorithmic Implementation}
\label{sec:algorithmic-implementation}
\idx{Algorithmic Implementation}

The following algorithms operationalize the post-biological transition axioms (Ax. \ref{ax:PB1}–\ref{ax:PB6}) and the Copernican principle (Ax. \ref{ax:COP}) to detect synthetic civilizations and estimate consciousness probabilities.

\begin{algorithmic}[H]
	\Procedure{DetectPostBiological}{$C$}
	\State $t \gets \CivilizationAge{C}$ \Comment{Age of civilization (Def. \ref{def:civilizationage})}
	\State $\tau \gets \TransitionTime{C}$ \Comment{Transition period (Def. \ref{def:transitiontime})}
	\If{$\tau / t < \varepsilon$} \Comment{$\varepsilon \ll 1$ (Schneider–Shostak \cite{schneider2025})}
	\State \Return $\PostBiological{C}$ \Comment{Post-biological (Thm. \ref{thm:postbio-necessity})}
	\Else
	\State \Return $\neg \PostBiological{C}$
	\EndIf
	\EndProcedure
\end{algorithmic}

\begin{algorithmic}[H]
	\Procedure{EstimateConsciousnessProbability}{$C, \text{samples}$}
	\State $\text{count} \gets 0$
	\For{$i = 1$ to samples}
	\State $x \gets \text{RandomEntity}(C)$
	\If{$\IsSynthetic{x}$} \Comment{Ax. \ref{ax:PB2}: $\IsSynthetic{x} \rightarrow \IsSuperintelligent{x}$}
	\If{$\IsSuperintelligent{x}$} \Comment{Ax. \ref{ax:PB3}: $\IsSuperintelligent{x} \rightarrow \HasAwareness{x}$}
	\State $\text{count} \gets \text{count} + 1$ \Comment{$\HasAwareness{x} \leftrightarrow \IsConscious{x}$ (Ax. \ref{ax:PB4})}
	\EndIf
	\EndIf
	\EndFor
	\State \Return $\text{count} / \text{samples}$ \Comment{Probability $\approx 1$ for old $C$ (Thm. \ref{thm:copernican-consciousness})}
	\EndProcedure
\end{algorithmic}

\begin{algorithmic}[H]
	\Procedure{SimulateGreatFilter}{$N_0, \Delta t$}
	\State $C \gets \text{InitializeCivilization}(N_0)$ \Comment{Initial biological population}
	\For{$t = 1$ to $\Delta t$}
	\If{$\Technological{C}$ \textbf{and} $\mathsf{UncontrolledAI}(C)$} \Comment{Hypothesis: AI as Great Filter \cite{garrett2024}}
	\State $\mathsf{EliminatedByASI}(C) \gets \text{True}$
	\State \textbf{break} \Comment{Short window (Thm. \ref{thm:short-window})}
	\EndIf
	\State $\text{UpdateEvolution}(C)$
	\EndFor
	\State \Return $C$
	\EndProcedure
\end{algorithmic}
